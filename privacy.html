Privacy policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

"clipper" is a private, single-user tool run by the Bad Decisions channel. It uploads videos that we recorded ourselves to our own YouTube channel, Facebook Page and Instagram account.

What data it uses

  • OAuth access tokens for our own YouTube, Facebook and Instagram accounts, used only to upload our own videos and their titles, descriptions and tags.
  • No data from any other user is requested, collected, stored or shared. The tool has no public sign-in.

How it is stored

Tokens are stored only on our own computer and are never sold, shared or sent anywhere other than the Google (YouTube Data API) and Meta (Graph API) servers they belong to.

Google user data

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It uses the YouTube Data API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google) or in Facebook Settings > Business Integrations (Meta).
